8. Errors — four patterns replace every alert()

Where an error appears depends on where you were: (a) on the row you tapped, (b) next to the button, (c) a box in place when the whole thing failed, (d) a toast with Retry when the thing you tapped is already gone (e.g. an Undo that failed). All wrap, all readable, none block the screen.

9. Settings → Ingredient groups — where a group came from

A small tag shows whether a group is built in, came from a checklist merge, or was typed in by hand (no tag). Delete and Undo work as today, so a learned merge is reviewable and reversible here. Session-only merges never appear here — they only last for that shopping list (and have Undo right after).
