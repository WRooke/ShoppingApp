Settings — plain language

Every Settings form that defines a rename, swap or conversion now reads left → right in words, with a live preview and a stated scope.

1. Settings index — say what each section is for

Each section gets a one-line purpose and the number says what it counts. One name per feature (the page used to be called “Substitutions” in the list and “Saved ingredient swaps” inside; “Ingredient groups”, “Coarse” and “Product units” were jargon).

Direction is stated in words (“When a recipe says … show it as …”), the two amounts are one readable sentence, the unit rule is explained where it applies, and a live preview sentence says exactly what will happen. Half-filled amounts show an inline “fill both or leave blank” instead of silently being thrown away.
